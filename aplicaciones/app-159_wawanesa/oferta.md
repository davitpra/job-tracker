About The Wawanesa Mutual Insurance Company

Founded in 1896, The Wawanesa Mutual Insurance Company is one of Canada’s largest mutual insurers, fully owned by its more than 1.8 million members. Headquartered in Winnipeg, Wawanesa is the parent company of WSI™, a specialty insurer delivering tailored solutions built around unique needs; Wawanesa Life, a provider of life insurance products; and Western Financial Group, a leading national distributor of personal and commercial insurance.

Wawanesa is committed to helping communities thrive in a changing world. The company invests more than $4 million annually in charitable initiatives, including $2.5 million each year to support people and communities on the front lines of climate change. Learn more at wawanesa.com.

Direct to Member (D2M) is a new initiative at Wawanesa focused on finding better, simpler ways for members to access insurance. We're looking for people excited to collaborate, test new ideas and explore new ways of working. By learning what works and using technology to make things easier, we're helping shape the future of how we serve our members.

Job Overview

Working under minimal supervision, this job contributes to Wawanesa's success by designing, building, and enhancing customer-facing digital experiences, with a strong emphasis on modern, accessible, and bilingual (English/French) front-end solutions. The role works in a collaborative agile team, delivering iteratively and following an AI-first approach to development that accelerates delivery while upholding high standards of code quality, security, and maintainability.

Design, develop, and test software collaborating with business analysts and stakeholders using an AI-first development approach.

Build and enhance responsive, customer-facing digital experiences using modern front-end technologies. 
Develop reusable UI components within a shared design system, supporting bilingual (English/French) user experiences. 
Integrate front-end applications with backend services and APIs.
